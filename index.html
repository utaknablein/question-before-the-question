The Question Before the Question

AI is built to answer what you ask. The expensive mistake is rarely a wrong answer. It is a fluent answer to the wrong question. Vorfrage checks the question first.

In German law, a Vorfrage is the preliminary question a court must settle before it can rule on the main one.

How it works: the seven shifts

Every reframe moves the question in one of seven directions. In Live with AI, Vorfrage picks the three that change it most. In Reframe it yourself, you answer the shifts and choose.

Why this matters now

AI made answers nearly free. That moves the scarce input upstream, to the question. Models can often detect a flawed premise and still answer anyway, because answering is what they are rewarded for. Vorfrage makes the check a visible step, keeps the person in charge of the choice, and records which reframes change decisions.
